# HEStats Explains: How UK Higher Education Works

## Caption

Most people see universities as places with lectures, degrees and graduation ceremonies.

But a university is also a research organisation, major employer, property owner, public-service institution and regulated provider operating with complex finances.

That complexity matters.

Tuition fees, international fees, research grants, public funding, commercial income, cash, borrowing and regulation all shape how the system works.

This is why HEStats exists: to make higher education easier to understand through structured public data.

## Short Caption

UK universities are not just lecture halls.

They are teaching institutions, research organisations, employers, property owners and regulated public-interest bodies.

To understand higher education, you have to follow the money, the data and the regulation.
