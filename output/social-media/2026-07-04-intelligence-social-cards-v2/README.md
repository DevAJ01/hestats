# HEStats Intelligence Social Cards V2

Design-polished, output-only carousel pack. No application source files were changed.

## What changed

- More consistent spacing between headline, metric, body and footer
- Dedicated metric panel on every card
- Shorter body blocks on chart-heavy cards
- Compact row charts and stat chips with safer footer spacing
- Cleaner contact sheet with no caption collisions

## Contents

- 14 square social cards as PNG and SVG masters
- `manifest.json` with card text, metrics and source labels
- `review-manifest.json` for the review renderer
- `review-board.html`, `intelligence-social-contact-sheet.png` and widget payload files when generated

## Suggested caption

AI is not just a tech story. It is a graduate jobs story.

26% of UK businesses were already using at least one AI technology in March 2026, but only 5% of AI-using businesses reported headcount reductions. The bigger risk for graduates is task change: which degrees lead into high-exposure industries, which sectors still need human judgement, and whether universities are preparing students for the work they will actually do.

Sources: ONS, ILO, DfE LEO, HESA Graduate Outcomes, HEStats Intelligence.

## Provenance notes

- Official statistics and source metadata come from the local HEStats Intelligence data pipeline as of 1 July 2026.
- AI exposure and degree automation-risk figures are HEStats modelling fields over DfE LEO-derived graduate destination data; they are not official job-loss forecasts.
- ONS BICS AI measures are official statistics in development and should be framed with caution.
- ILO and other global labour-market exposure figures are external analysis, not UK official graduate outcome statistics.
