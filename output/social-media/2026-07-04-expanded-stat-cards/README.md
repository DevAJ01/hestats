## Caption

The alarming statistic is not one university. It is the pattern.

Verified FY2024-25 finance rows loaded in HEStats show deficits, tight liquidity, borrowing pressure and year-on-year margin deterioration across the provider base.

113 of 296 filtered verified provider rows are in deficit. Among £100m+ income providers, 50 of 120 are in deficit. 24 providers sit below 30 liquidity days.

Source: HESA finance tables via HEStats. Pending rows and rows failing basic claim-safety checks excluded.
