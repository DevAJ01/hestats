# HEStats product and data audit — 26 July 2026

## General health

The product is now in a credible, usable state for research and exploration. Trust-critical score bounds, source labels, missing-data handling, keyboard access, and primary navigation have been corrected. The main remaining engineering concern is delivery performance: the production JavaScript bundle is 777 kB gzip and should be split before a high-traffic launch.

## Findings and resolutions

1. **Critical — financial health scores were outside the declared 0–100 scale.** The same normalization error appeared across the overview, rankings, and map, including values above 1,000 and negative grades. The normalization now returns a bounded ratio, with regression tests covering all components.
   - Evidence: [overview before](2026-07-26-before/01-overview.jpg), [overview after](2026-07-26-after/01-overview.jpg), [comparison](2026-07-26-after/comparison-overview.jpg)

2. **High — the rankings page did not answer the core “best overall institution” question.** It only exposed single financial measures. An evidence-coverage-aware overall score now combines finance, graduate outcomes, research intensity, and estate energy efficiency. Missing dimensions are reweighted and coverage is always visible.
   - Evidence: [rankings before](2026-07-26-before/02-rankings.jpg), [rankings after](2026-07-26-after/03-rankings-overall.jpg), [comparison](2026-07-26-after/comparison-rankings.jpg)

3. **High — the map was hover-dependent and did not retain an institution selection.** It now provides selectable evidence layers, a legend, click-to-pin behavior, a persistent detail panel, URL state, campus-coordinate precision, and Tab/Enter/Space support.
   - Evidence: [map before](2026-07-26-before/03-map.jpg), [map after](2026-07-26-after/04-map-overall.jpg), [comparison](2026-07-26-after/comparison-map.jpg)

4. **High — Nottingham’s current year was pending and its timeline mixed generic narrative with unattributed events.** The signed 2024/25 accounts are now represented as a verified provider record. The timeline is generated from sourced finance, HESA estates, and TEF records, with explicit status and source links.
   - Evidence: [timeline before](2026-07-26-before/04-nottingham-timeline.jpg), [timeline after](2026-07-26-after/05-nottingham-timeline.jpg), [comparison](2026-07-26-after/comparison-timeline.jpg)

5. **High — estates coverage used a small set of uploaded extracts and did not preserve the complete published structure.** The official DT042 Tables 1–5 archive is now retained as a 346,050-row gzip download and ingested into provider-year summaries plus normalized latest-year metrics. Suppressed, blank, optional, and unmatched provider rows remain explicit.
   - Evidence: [Nottingham estates](2026-07-26-after/06-nottingham-estates.jpg), [open-data archive](2026-07-26-after/07-open-data-estates.jpg)

6. **Medium — sector risk was fragmented across isolated statistics.** A transparent four-component System Watch now combines institutional finance, graduate employment, UK labour demand, and recruitment exposure. It is explicitly an early-warning indicator, not a forecast or credit rating.
   - Evidence: [System Watch](2026-07-26-after/02-system-watch.jpg)

7. **Medium — high-value student and intelligence journeys were hidden while support prompts occupied prime space.** System Watch and Students now have direct navigation, the home screen presents clear research workflows, and the contribution prompt has been moved out of the primary decision area.

## Data coverage and limitations

- Platform directory: 304 institutions and providers.
- Verified 2024/25 finance: 299 providers; five remain explicitly pending.
- Full HESA estates archive: 346,050 data rows, Tables 1–5, academic years 2015/16–2024/25.
- Matched estates summaries: 1,422 provider-year records; 2,599 normalized headline metrics for the latest year.
- Seven historical HESA UKPRNs in the archive do not have a safe canonical match in the current platform directory and remain unmatched.
- HESA Estates participation and field completion are not universal. Missing or suppressed values are never converted to zero.

## Verification

- TypeScript typecheck passed.
- 84 unit and integration tests passed.
- 28 data-validation tests passed.
- Production build and 324-route SEO prerender passed.
- Browser QA covered the overview, rankings, System Watch, map click and keyboard selection, Nottingham timeline and estates, and full archive handoff.

## Remaining recommendations

1. Code-split route and generated-data bundles before production scale.
2. Add a scheduled source-refresh job with publication-date and schema-drift alerts.
3. Resolve unmatched historical UKPRNs only through a reviewed provider crosswalk.
4. Add authentication, entitlements, metering, and billing later around the stable API resources; no paywall was introduced in this change.
